# 画风对比样图提示词

同一个原创角色「行脚僧」，四种画风各出一张待机立绘（透明底）。共用的角色描述：

```text
Original character "Wandering Monk" for a Chinese-mythology mobile auto-battler. Young male monk, shaved head, calm and determined face, saffron-yellow inner robe, deep red kasaya draped over the left shoulder with gold trim, a string of large dark wooden prayer beads around the neck, a tall bronze ring staff with six rings held upright in the right hand, straw sandals, a small cloth bundle tied on the back. Full body, standing idle pose, three-quarter view facing right, weight slightly forward, ready to fight. One single character, centered, full body inside the frame with generous margin, fully transparent background, no ground, no shadow on the floor, no text, no border.
```

## qban — Q版厚描边

```text
Style: 2.5-head-tall chibi proportions, thick dark brown outlines, flat cel shading with one shadow tone and one highlight tone, bright saturated colors, big clean shapes that stay readable when the sprite is only 64 pixels tall. Image 1 is a style reference only: match its outline weight, shading and proportions, but do not copy any of its characters.
```

## piying — 皮影

```text
Style: traditional Chinese shadow puppet (piying). The figure is made of translucent dyed leather pieces: crisp black carved outlines, carved openwork patterns (clouds, lotus) inside the robe, a limited palette of red, yellow, green and black dye. Visible round rivet joints at shoulder, elbow, hip and knee, with each limb a separate flat piece. Head in strict side profile like a real shadow puppet while the body is turned three-quarter. Slightly elongated, about 3.5 heads tall. Lit from behind so the leather glows warm and translucent.
```

## shuimo — 水墨Q版

```text
Style: Chinese ink-wash chibi, about 2.5 heads tall. Loose but confident black brush-stroke outlines with varying thickness and dry-brush texture, soft watercolor washes for color that bleed slightly past the lines, a muted elegant palette (ochre, vermilion, indigo, warm grey). No paper texture in the background: the background stays fully transparent.
```

## pixel — 像素

```text
Style: pixel-art game sprite, 2.5-head-tall chibi. Drawn on a 64x64 pixel grid and scaled up 16x with nearest-neighbor so every pixel is a crisp square block, 1-pixel dark outline, a limited 16-color palette, no anti-aliasing, no gradients, no blur.
```
